Privacy Policy for Luma Cycle

Effective date: 2026-10-28

Luma Cycle is a local-only period tracking app.

Data Collection

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers. All period data, journal entries, symptoms, mood, and settings are stored only on your device.

Permissions

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android) - Used only if you enable period reminders
  • USE_BIOMETRIC - Used only if you enable app lock

Data Sharing

No data is shared automatically. If you choose to export data, the app creates a JSON file that you can share manually via your device's share sheet. Exported files are unencrypted - only share with trusted people.

Backups

The app disables system backups where possible. Your device's backup behavior is controlled by your OS settings.

Third Parties

No third-party analytics, ads, tracking, or crash reporting.

Changes

This policy may be updated. Changes will be reflected in this document.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the app store listing.
