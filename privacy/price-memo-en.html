Privacy Policy — Price Memo

Effective date: November 26, 2026

Price Memo (application ID: com.livduo.pricememo) is a personal price memory and comparison tool that works offline on your device.

1. Data collection

We do not collect, transmit, or store personally identifiable information on external servers. The app has no accounts, no backend, no advertising, and no behavioral analytics.

The Google ML Kit component on Android may send app and device information, installation identifiers, feature events, and performance diagnostics to Google over HTTPS for SDK operation, diagnostics, and usage analytics. Photos, decoded barcodes, and recognized text are processed on-device.

2. Data stored on your device

All data — your product catalog (name, brand, category, barcode, package size, notes), stores, recorded price observations (price, shelf/purchase kind, promotions, source), target-price watchlist entries, and receipt imports — is stored entirely locally on your device in the app's own SQLite database.

This data never leaves your device unless you deliberately export or back it up, or an SDK provider sends diagnostics as described in sections 1 and 5. You have full control: edit, archive, or delete individual items in the app, or wipe everything by uninstalling. Android Auto Backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled (android:allowBackup="false").

3. Camera and on-device OCR

The app requests camera permission only when you choose to scan a barcode, photograph a price tag, or photograph a receipt. Photos you capture or pick are processed on-device by Google ML Kit — image content, decoded barcodes, and OCR text are not sent to our servers. You always review and confirm OCR results before anything is saved; the app never persists OCR output automatically.

Images used for OCR are temporary files and are deleted after processing unless you choose to keep them. Product or evidence photos you deliberately retain stay inside the app's private storage.

4. Backup, export and sharing

You can deliberately create a backup (database, settings, and app-managed media files) and share or save it through the system dialog. Backup files are not additionally encrypted by the app. When you share, data may leave Price Memo's storage area and becomes subject to the recipient's policies. The app never sends, syncs, or uploads this data anywhere by itself.

5. System permissions

The Android release declares: the camera permission (android.permission.CAMERA) for the scanning/OCR features — requested only when you use them; and network access (android.permission.INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, declared by the Google ML Kit library) used for SDK operation, fetching resources when needed, and sending SDK diagnostics/performance data to Google over HTTPS. The app does not request location (GPS), contacts, microphone, notifications, or other sensitive permissions, and never sends user data over the network itself.

6. Advertising and third-party services

The app contains no advertising, no tracking SDKs, no behavioral analytics, and no third-party crash reporting. Google ML Kit may send app and device information, installation identifiers, and performance diagnostics to Google over HTTPS as described in sections 1 and 5.

7. Children

The app is a personal shopping price tool and is not directed at collecting information from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or about Price Memo, please contact us at livduocom@gmail.com.
