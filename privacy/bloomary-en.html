Privacy Policy — Bloomary – Plant Journal

Effective date: November 17, 2026

Bloomary – Plant Journal (package: com.livduo.bloomary) is a personal plant journal that works entirely offline on your device — no account, no cloud, no ads.

1. Data collection

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information about you on external servers. The app has no account system, no backend server, no ads, no behavioral analytics, and does not request internet access (no android.permission.INTERNET permission is declared).

2. Data stored on your device

All data — plant profiles (name, scientific name, category, variety, acquisition date/price, tags, notes, cover photo), care journal entries, growth measurements, health observations, reminders and settings — is stored entirely locally on your device through the app's own SQLite database.

The app also stores locally a random installation ID, the app version, and launch times/counts for app-lifecycle tracking. This data never leaves your device. You have full control: edit, archive, or delete individual plants and journal entries in the app, or erase everything by uninstalling. Android system auto-backup is disabled (android:allowBackup="false").

3. Photos and media

Photos you import (from gallery or camera) are stored privately on your device and used only to illustrate your own journal entries. Plant records reference media by ID; photo bytes are never duplicated. The app never uploads photos anywhere.

4. Backup, restore and sharing

You can proactively create a backup file (database, allowlisted settings and media files) on your device, then share it yourself through the system share sheet when you choose. Backup files are not encrypted — the checksum only detects corruption, it does not authenticate the source. Share only with people you trust. The app never sends, syncs, or uploads this file anywhere and receives no copies.

5. System permissions

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — used only for plant care reminders you create yourself.
  • Camera — only when you choose to take a photo for a plant or journal entry.
  • Photo/media access — only when you choose to import an existing photo (Android uses the system photo picker).
  • Exact alarm scheduling (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM) — for on-time reminders; an inexact fallback is used when the system does not allow it.
  • Boot completed (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) — to re-arm notifications after device reboot.

6. Ads and third parties

The app contains no advertising, no behavioral tracking SDKs, no third-party analytics, and no third-party crash reporting.

7. Children

The app is a personal journal tool for adults and is not directed at collecting personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Policy changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be reflected by updating the effective date in this document.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or about Bloomary, please contact us at: livduocom@gmail.com.
