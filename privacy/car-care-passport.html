Privacy Policy for Car Care Passport

Effective date: 2026-11-14

Car Care Passport is a private, offline-first vehicle maintenance log.

Data Collection

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers. All vehicle profiles, maintenance records, care task schedules, costs, notes, and app settings are stored only on your device.

Data Stored on Your Device

  • Vehicle profiles you enter (name, make/model, plate number, odometer readings)
  • Maintenance records you create (dates, odometer, costs, notes)
  • Photos and PDF receipts you attach to records
  • Care task definitions and due-date schedules
  • App preferences (language, theme, reminder settings)

Permissions

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android) - Used only if you enable maintenance reminders
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED (Android) - Used to reschedule local reminders after a device reboot

Data Sharing

No data is shared automatically. If you choose to create a backup or export a PDF record, the app produces a file that you control and can share manually via your device's share sheet.

Your Control

You can delete any vehicle, record, or attachment inside the app; uninstalling the app removes all app data. Backups you export are under your control.

Third Parties

No third-party analytics, ads, tracking, or crash reporting. The app requires no internet connection.

Changes

This policy may be updated. Changes will be reflected in this document.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please contact livduocom@gmail.com.
