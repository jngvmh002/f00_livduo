Scope

Sales Diary (Sales Diary, com.livduo.salesdiary) is developed by LivDuo. This policy describes how the app handles data.

Data and purposes

The app stores locally: sales and purchase transactions, products, prices, quantities, notes, stock adjustments, customer and supplier catalogs, product categories, units of measure, and payments. This data is entered by you for record-keeping and sales reporting. Customer and supplier names and notes may contain personal information depending on what you enter.

The app stores your language and theme preferences. It also stores locally a random install ID, app version, timestamps, and open counts to track install lifecycle on the device.

The app requires no account, integrates no advertising or behavioral analytics, and does not send the records above to LivDuo.

Export and transfer

You can choose where to save JSON backup files or Excel reports via Android's file picker. Exported files are unencrypted. If you choose cloud-managed storage, share a file, or open a report in another spreadsheet app, that provider may process the data under its own policy. When you open the policy link, the browser loads LivDuo's web page. The app does not attach your sales records to the link.

Retention and deletion

On-device data is kept until you edit or delete it using the app's functions, replace all data with a restore file, clear the app's data in Android, or uninstall. Copies and reports you export are stored separately; delete them at their storage location and in any services or apps you transferred files to.
