Privacy Policy — Recipe Cost

Effective date: November 15, 2026

Recipe Cost (application ID: com.livduo.recipecost) is an offline recipe/material cost and selling-price calculator for small F&B and manufacturing businesses. It runs entirely on your device.

1. Data collection

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information on external servers. The app has no account system, no backend server, no advertising, no behavioral analytics, and does not request Internet access (android.permission.INTERNET).

2. Data stored on your device

All business data — your business profile (name, type, currency, language, unit system, target margin/markup, fees and taxes), materials and purchase-price history, products/recipes and components, and quotes with line items — is stored entirely locally on your device in the app's own SQLite database.

This data never leaves your device. You have full control: edit or delete individual items inside the app, or erase everything by uninstalling the app. Android Auto Backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled (android:allowBackup="false").

3. Bundled reference catalog

The app ships with a read-only sample catalog of ingredients/products (e.g., pizza, coffee, bubble tea). This catalog is static data bundled inside the app; it contains no personal data and does not change based on anything you enter.

4. Backup, export, and sharing

You can proactively create a JSON backup file, export data as JSON or CSV, or generate a PDF quote and share it — then choose the destination through the operating system's dialog. These files are not encrypted by the app. When you pick a save location or a receiving app/service, the data may leave Recipe Cost's storage area and is then subject to that destination's own policies and security. The app never sends, syncs, or uploads these files anywhere by itself.

5. System permissions

The Android release build of this app declares no special permissions: no Internet, location (GPS), contacts, camera, microphone, notifications, or other sensitive permissions. It only uses local storage and system dialogs that you open yourself.

6. Advertising and third-party services

The app contains no advertising, no behavioral tracking SDKs, no analytics, and no third-party crash reporting.

7. Children

The app is a business calculation tool for adults and is not intended to collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or the Recipe Cost app, please contact us at livduocom@gmail.com.
