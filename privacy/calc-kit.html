Privacy Policy for Công Cụ Tính Toán Đa Năng

Effective date: 2026-10-29

Công Cụ Tính Toán Đa Năng ("calc_kit") is a local-only calculation app for everyday financial and household calculations.

Data Collection

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers. The app has no account system, no backend, and no network permission.

Data Stored on Your Device

Calculation history and pinned tools are stored only on your device using Android's local app storage. History is limited to the 50 most recent successful calculations. This data never leaves your device and is not shared with us or any third party.

You can delete individual history entries in the app, clear all history from the history screen, or remove all local data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Permissions

The app does not request internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or other sensitive permissions.

Data Sharing

No data is shared automatically. The app does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs.

Third Parties

No third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or data-broker services are used.

Children

The app is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

This policy may be updated when app behavior changes. The effective date above will be updated accordingly.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the Google Play store listing for Công Cụ Tính Toán Đa Năng.
